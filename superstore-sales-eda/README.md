# Superstore Sales Data Analysis Project


## Objective
Analyze Superstore sales data to identify key trends, profitability drivers, and business improvement opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Key Business Questions

- Which regions and states generate the highest sales?
- What are the key profit drivers and loss areas?
- How do discounts impact profitability?
- What are the sales trends over time?
- Which categories and products perform best?

## Key Insights

- West region generates the highest sales overall.
- Consumer segment is the largest contributor to revenue.
- Technology category drives both high sales and profit.
- Furniture category shows lower profitability and higher losses.
- High discounts are strongly correlated with losses.
- Some states (Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania) consistently generate losses.
- Sales peaked in 2017 with strong growth trends.
- Quarter 4 shows highest sales due to seasonal demand.
- Top-performing products significantly contribute to overall revenue.
- Average shipping time is approximately 3 days.

## Key Metrics
          
- Total Sales: $2297200.86
- Total Profit: $286397.02
- Average Discount: 0.16
- Average Shipping Days: 3.96 days
           
## Project Workflow
- Data Cleaning
- Feature Engineering
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business Insights Extraction

 # Conclusion

This analysis highlights that while sales are strong in certain regions and categories, profitability is impacted by high discounts 
and underperforming segments. Strategic discount control and focus on profitable categories can improve overall business performance.

## Business Recommendations

- Reduce high discount strategies to improve profitability
- Focus on Technology category for higher returns
- Investigate loss-making states for operational inefficiencies
- Optimize shipping methods to reduce delivery time
